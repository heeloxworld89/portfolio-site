Dear Goodfire team,

In February, Tom McGrath wrote that AI should be intentionally designed, not discovered by trial and error. I got into research because of that gap. Building a multi-agent LLM system, I trained my own models, and when one got worse I could not see where or why. I wanted to look inside, so I went back to the fundamentals.

The result I am proudest of is a finding about model internals that I tried hard to break. A theorem from my sole-author DeepMath 2026 paper says that bounded per-channel repairs can never revive a dead BatchNorm--ReLU channel. I turned it into a check and ran it on torchvision's VGG-19-BN: 292 channels output exactly zero on all 10,000 ImageNetV2 photos, 18.6\% of the model's compute. Removing them changed none of the predictions. Removing 292 random live channels instead changed 499 of 500. That control is why I trusted the result enough to publish it, with code, raw data and its limits stated (\href{https://www.raadh.me/dead-channels}{raadh.me/dead-channels}).

I have completed one full research cycle on my own: theory, 383 seeded experiments on a single GPU, a peer-reviewed paper (accepted alongside papers from Ren\'e Vidal's and Richard Baraniuk's groups), an open-source tool, \href{https://github.com/Oxiedo/ormas-monitor}{ormas-monitor}, and an invited talk at Cohere Labs, where I presented corrections to my own results. I also review for the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Trustworthy AI for Good, on interpretability, attribution and model auditing.

What I have not had is a team, frontier models, or mentors who helped build this field. Your recent work on probes as fast monitors and on using interpretability to shape training is where my work points next: from detecting dead units in CNNs to understanding what changes inside a foundation model while it trains. I execute well-defined plans quickly, I take feedback well, and I would give the three months everything.

To be direct about logistics: I would need visa sponsorship, and I understand it is an exception. I am ready to relocate to San Francisco and work full-time in person. If you can consider me, I believe the work above makes the case.

Thank you for reading.

Rokib Al Dhin Raadh
