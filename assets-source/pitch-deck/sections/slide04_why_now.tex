    \textit{The requirement is no longer performance. It is provenance.}}

    \amb{Which means training on your own data --- the thing nobody can do safely.}}
  \column{0.33\textwidth}
  \colcard[2.17cm]{MODELS DEGRADE INVISIBLY}{%

    \textit{It surfaces as a miscalculation, not an alert.}}
