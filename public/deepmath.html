The takeaway

Small repairs keep training stable. Only a jump can revive a dead layer.

We prove both, which makes self-repair a switched system: bounded flicks between isolated jumps.

Three numbers

  • 10.00%without repair, after two conv layers are zeroed: exactly chance, 99 epochs, 3/3 seeds.
  • 39 493the training step where the first reset fires, identical in every seed.
  • 80.3%with self-repair (85% before the lesion).

Gradient descent and small fixes are both stuck, exactly. Only a jump leaves DDD.

Open problem

How often may a network jump?

Proposition 1 holds between jumps; Theorem 2 says jumps are unavoidable. Find a dwell time τD\tau_DτD​ such that jumps at least τD\tau_DτD​ apart keep training stable (cf. Hespanha & Morse, 1999).

Also open: strong convexity cannot hold exactly for BatchNorm layers (scale invariance); Cui et al. (2025) give ISS under a generalised PL condition, but whether BN networks satisfy it is open.
